## Observation

During a Vacuum cast the template Magnet Effect bolts appear beside the hose and around the body, not at the
nozzle (the rigid head at the top of the hose).

## Reproduction

1. Instantiate `Prefabs/Boosters/Effects/VacuumEffect.prefab` with its root turned to a pitch-75 camera, as
   `BoardPresenter.StartVacuum` places it.
2. Sample the motion at 0.5 and simulate the Magnet Effect at `ReadyIntakePosition` (what `VacuumEffectView.Play`
   does).
3. Render the camera: the bolts sit left and right of the hose, below the head.

## Expected and actual

- Expected: the suction feedback targets the nozzle tip, where the pieces go (AC-2, AC-9, DEC-152).
- Actual: the particle's circle shape (radius 1.57, shape rotation X -90) lies in the root's right/forward plane,
  edge-on to the camera, and is wider than the whole vacuum, so the bolts spread beside the hose.
