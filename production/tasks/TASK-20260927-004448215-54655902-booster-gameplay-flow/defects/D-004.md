## Observation

The blocks a Vacuum pulls do not visibly reach the nozzle tip: they shrink to nothing while still over the board.

## Reproduction

1. Use Vacuum on a colour with blocks in the upper half of the board.
2. Watch a pulled block during the intake window.
3. By the time it is near the vacuum it is a few pixels wide; it disappears over the board, not at the nozzle.

## Expected and actual

- Expected: each piece flies into the nozzle while it shrinks and is seen entering it (AC-9).
- Actual: `BoardPresenter.StartRemoval` tweened position and scale with the same `Ease.InQuad`, so the remaining
  scale equals the remaining distance fraction: 25 % size at a quarter of the way left, 5 % at 5 %. A pitch-75
  preview (scratchpad blk_strip.png) shows the block invisible while still inside the board area.
