# Final Ice reference comparison

Root and a separate read-only visual reviewer inspected the unchanged [user target](dense-reference-target.png), the [final actual Preview 4](reference-calibration-final-cyan4.png), and the [final actual Game portrait](reference-match3-final-runtime-portrait.png).

- **Numeral: PASS.** Heavy pale-cyan fill, navy outline and the 4's proportions closely follow the target. The Game image shows readable 1, 4, 6, 10 and 20. The single-cell 20 has a tight but contained margin and no apparent clipping.
- **Rings: PASS.** Broad mint-cyan bevel rings replace the earlier thin white highlights. Minor lower shadows remain consistent with the different camera framing and reference blur.
- **Body and texture: PASS.** Saturated blue-cyan coverage and subdued supplied-texture detail reproduce the reference's visual emphasis. Photographic facets no longer compete with the count.
- **Lower band: PASS.** A distinct blue side band and cyan rim replace the pale white skirt. Exact edge softness cannot be equated to the lower-resolution reference.
- **Footprint: PASS in the shown Game image.** The concave boundary and ring hole remain open; the material appears consistent across square, narrow, tall and holed blocks.

The saved material and rebuilt Preview shell agree at tint (0.14, 0.73, 0.98), highlight (0.48, 0.95, 1) and opacity 0.98. The generator uses supplied-texture strength 0.06–0.12 and a normal-based bevel mask with cap fade 0.90–0.999. Extra Fresnel glow is suppressed on vertical walls. The shared texture, panel, font and mesh assets were not changed by this refinement.

This is independent visual verification of these concrete captures, not explicit user acceptance or task closure. Current tall-aspect, fade/clipping and focused-test results belong in the separate final verification report. The earlier `reference-final-*` and `matched-final-*` images remain unchanged historical checkpoints and must not be treated as this final appearance.
