## Observation

After editing a Square template with the Pen and pressing "Discard template change", the blocks are not where they were: B1 and B2 overlap and both labels are struck through.

## Reproduction

1. Level 1 with two Square blocks; pick up the Pen and left click b2.
2. Add cells to the left or below (the bounding box grows left or down); every Square block follows.
3. Press "Discard template change".
4. The blocks take the Square footprint again, but anchored at the grown bounding-box corner, so they sit one cell off and overlap B1.

## Expected and actual

- Expected: every block the edit began with is back at its exact origin with its exact cells; only blocks placed during the edit are re-stamped.
- Actual: `LevelTemplateCommands.TryDiscard` re-stamps every block with `LevelShapeRestamp.TryRestamp`, which keeps each block's rotation or mirror but anchors it at its current (grown) bounding-box corner.
