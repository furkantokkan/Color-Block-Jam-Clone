A frame never becomes `LastPublishedLevel`, and when playback ends or stops the preview shows the draft again. A frame the preview cannot show keeps the previous frame and warns once without interrupting the window. Clicking the playing row stops it; clicking another row plays that entry from its start.

A press on the canvas while an entry plays stops the playback and edits nothing. Playback stops, and the canvas and the preview show the draft (after a switch, the new level), on: its end, the row's stop, another row, a canvas press, any draft change, undo or redo, a level or JSON switch, Save Level, Validate Level or Test Level, entering Play Mode, the window's `OnDisable` and a domain reload.
