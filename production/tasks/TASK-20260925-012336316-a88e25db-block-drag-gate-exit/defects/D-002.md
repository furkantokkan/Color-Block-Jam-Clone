## Observation

A Failed subscriber may restart successfully yet end with Playing and no attempt.

## Reproduction

1. Prepare and play a two-block fixture with an expiring countdown.
2. Subscribe to Failed and call Restart from that callback.
3. Tick the session, then inspect the replacement attempt and run the next tick.

## Expected and actual

- Expected: Restart preserves its new active attempt after the Failed callback returns.
- Actual: EnterFailed tears down the replacement attempt after notifying subscribers.
