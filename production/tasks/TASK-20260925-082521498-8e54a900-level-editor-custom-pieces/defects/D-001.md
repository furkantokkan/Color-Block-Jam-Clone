## Observation

While drawing a piece on the board, its centre (the B3 label) is placed wrongly on the right side; the user reports it looks right after selecting again, but not during the first drawing.

## Reproduction

1. Draw a 7-shaped piece with "+ New piece": a three-cell top row and a four-cell column under its right end, next to a 2 x 2 block on its left.
2. Look at its label on the canvas.
3. The label "B3" is centred on the left edge of the column, so half of it lies over the neighbouring block B1.

## Expected and actual

- Expected: the id label lies on the block (scenario 18).
- Actual: `LevelCanvasElement.GetLabelCentre` accepts the footprint mean whenever the point touches a block cell; the 7 shape's mean (2.07, 1.93 in cell units) lies 0.07 cells inside its column, so the two-cell-wide label straddles B1.
