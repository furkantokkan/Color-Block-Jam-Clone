## Observation

When a gate next to a board corner sinks, the corner piece's arm that touched the gate shows a flat, cut vertical end with a hard edge. In the reference game the corner is its own rounded unit: its arm end next to the gate is rounded, like the end of a wall brick.

## Reproduction

1. Play level 1 in the Game scene (or the capture harness) at 1080x1920.
2. Lean or exit the cyan block through the top gate, whose right end touches the top-right corner.
3. While the gate is down, look at the top-right corner's left end: it is a flat face, not a rounded end.

## Expected and actual

- Expected: every corner reads as a separate rounded unit; with the gate lowered, its arm end is rounded as in references user-1 and user-5.
- Actual: the supplied Corner_1 arm ends flat (it was made to butt against a Wall), and the lowered gate uncovers that face.
