## Observation

At the board's outer corners, where the straight ring wall pieces meet the rounded corner piece, a thin line (break, crease) crosses the wall top and the surfaces look slightly stepped. The same hairline seams show at all four outer corners and under the top gate at 1080 x 1920; 540 x 960 captures cannot resolve them.

## Reproduction

1. Open level 1 in the Game scene (Play Mode) or in the LevelEditor scene preview.
2. View or capture the board at 1080 x 1920 (the target resolution).
3. Look at each outer ring corner and the join under a gate.

## Expected and actual

- Expected: ring walls and corners join without a visible line or step, like the reference game (AC-9).
- Actual: a hairline seam and a slight crease at every wall/corner join and under the gate.
